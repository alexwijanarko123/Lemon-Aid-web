Frequently Asked
Questions (FAQ)

1. What is this app?

This app helps you make smarter purchasing decisions and avoid impulsive buying through AI-powered product analysis, spending insights, and financial planning tools.

2. How does the AI purchase analysis work?

Simply enter or select a product you are considering buying. The AI will analyze the product based on factors such as its price, necessity, value, and potential impact on your spending habits.

3. Can I trust the AI recommendations?

AI recommendations are designed to help you evaluate your purchasing decisions. However, they are suggestions rather than financial advice. The final purchasing decision is always yours.

4. How many times can I use AI features per day?

Free users have a limited number of daily AI requests, while Premium users receive a higher daily usage limit.

5. What are the benefits of Premium?

Premium unlocks additional features, including:

  • Higher daily AI usage limits.
  • More detailed AI purchase insights.
  • AI-generated spending habit summaries.
  • Saving Forecast simulations.

6. How does AI Spending Habit Insights work?

The feature analyzes your recorded spending data to identify spending patterns, highlight unnecessary expenses, and provide personalized suggestions to improve your financial habits.

7. What is Saving Forecast?

Saving Forecast allows you to simulate how much money you could save over time by reducing or eliminating certain expenses. It helps you visualize the potential growth of your savings based on different spending scenarios.

8. Is my financial data safe?

Your financial information should only be used to support the app’s features and provide personalized insights. We prioritize user privacy and data protection.

9. Can I cancel my Premium subscription?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your account or the platform where you purchased it, subject to the applicable subscription terms.

10. What should I do if the app is not working properly?

Try restarting the application and checking your internet connection. If the problem persists, contact our support team for further assistance.

11. What if my AI request fails?

Check your internet connection and try again. If the issue continues, please contact our support team.

12. How can I contact customer support?

If you need further assistance or encounter an issue, you can reach our support team through the Contact Support section in the app.
